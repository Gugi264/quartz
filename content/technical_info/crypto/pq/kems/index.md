---
title: KEMs
---

A KEM (key encapsulation mechnsim) is a public-key cryptosystem that allows the sender to randomly generate a short secret key and transmit it to the receiver securely.

It acts as a replacement for public-key encryption (like RSAES-PKCS1, Elgmal, ...).
