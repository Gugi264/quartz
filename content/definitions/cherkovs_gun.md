---
title: Cherkov's gun
---

Is a dramatic principle that states that every element in a story must be necessary, and irrelevant elements should be removed. Elements should not appear to make "false promises" by never coming into play. The statement is recorded in letters by [Anton Chekhov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Chekhov) several times, with some variation.

Opposite of a [Red Herring](red_herring.md)
